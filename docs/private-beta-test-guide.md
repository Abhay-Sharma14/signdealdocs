# SignDealDocs — private beta review guide

**Status:** Pre-launch test build. Do not upload identity cards, bank statements, real contracts, confidential client files, or payment information. Use fictional names and dummy documents only.

## Test as a business owner
1. Open the preview URL provided by the product owner (not the public production domain unless explicitly approved).
2. Register/sign in using a dedicated test email. Do not reuse a personal password.
3. Create a fictional proposal: client "Demo Client", project "Website Design", amount 100, and one required document "Demo Brief".
4. Review the AI-generated proposal for grammar, pricing, formatting and unsupported promises. Edit before publishing.
5. Copy the private deal link and open it in another browser or incognito window to simulate the client.
6. Review and sign the fictional proposal; verify that the signing action changes the deal status.
7. Upload a harmless sample PDF or PNG, not a real personal document. Verify the file appears in the vault.
8. Test the same steps on a mobile browser. If possible, try camera upload with a picture of a blank sheet of paper.
9. Check what happens with invalid links, unsupported file types, blank form fields and files larger than the stated limit.
10. Note every confusing label, grammar issue, broken button, layout issue or unexpected error.

## Important limitations
- This is not a legally certified or qualified electronic-signature service.
- WhatsApp and email buttons open a prefilled message; they do not automatically deliver it.
- DigiLocker, automatic payments, paid subscriptions and external cloud import are not live.
- A successful build does not establish that live database migrations, document storage or end-to-end workflows are working.
- Private deal links grant access to whoever possesses the link. Do not share them publicly.
